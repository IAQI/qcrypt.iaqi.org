    Travel information

    How to get to Ottawa

    The most convenient way to go to Ottawa is by air. Ottawa International Airport is located about 15 kilometers from Ottawa downtown. It is connected by a light train and taxies. Ottawa International Airport operates frequent flights with the major hubs Montreal Trudeau International Airport and Toronto Pearson Airport.

    There is also the option of coming by train, using Via Rail. Ottawa Train station is just 10 minutes from Ottawa downtown. It is connected by a light train and taxies.
